How to read this tool

Choose your market, barrier and sample size. Over counts digits strictly greater than the barrier; Under counts digits strictly less. The quote is formatted with the market’s supplied precision so trailing zeros count correctly.

The reference assumes each digit is equally likely: Over = (9 − barrier) ÷ 10; Under = barrier ÷ 10. This is a comparison model, not a verified future win rate. “Above-reference frequency” appears when the sample is at least 100 ticks and a side is at least two standard errors above that reference. Repeated checks and overlapping samples can produce chance patterns.

The experimental entry rule checks for at least 5 percentage points above reference in both the selected sample and the last 50 ticks. It needs the complete selected sample, a fresh live tick, and a 10-tick interval between triggers. Each indication expires after five seconds or the next received tick. Its following digit is recorded as a hit, miss, or missed observation.

Recent frequency and streaks do not establish an edge or predict the next digit. Contract payout and cost also determine profitability. This tool analyzes ticks; it does not place orders.

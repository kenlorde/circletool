How the analysis works

4-hour swing highs and lows are confirmed by three candles on each side. A 15-minute candle must touch a zone, close away from it, and confirm direction against the previous close. Zone width is 20% of the 14-candle average true range. The 4H EMA 20/50 trend and EMA 20 slope must agree with the M15 direction. Rejections require a clear wick, a strong close and a candle range between 0.5 and 2 ATR. Missing M15 candles during open sessions or unverified history block signals. Verified history is reused until a new candle closes, and rate-limited requests retry automatically. Gaps fully covered by verified Deriv trading-hour closures are allowed. After reopening, three completed consecutive M15 candles are required. Historical 4H candles may span market closures; those gaps do not block analysis.

Stops sit beyond the rejection and zone with a volatility buffer. Entries use the current quote and require at least 2:1 reward-to-risk before the opposing level. The entry window lasts three minutes after candle close, with a maximum drift of 20% of M15 ATR. Expired setups stay expired.

Deriv market data is separate from your MT5 broker. No account connection or automatic orders. This strategy has not been backtested and does not check economic news.

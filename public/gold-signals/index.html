How the analysis works

4-hour swing highs and lows are confirmed by three candles on each side. A 15-minute candle must touch a zone, close away from it, and confirm direction against the previous close. Zone width is 20% of the 14-candle average true range.

Stops sit beyond the rejection and zone with a volatility buffer. Targets aim for twice the risk, capped before the opposing level. Setups below 1.5 reward-to-risk are skipped. A signal expires at the next candle or when price moves too far, reaches the stop, or reaches the target.

Deriv market data is separate from your MT5 broker. No account connection or automatic orders. This strategy has not been backtested and does not check economic news.
